! Title: consent-rr - CookieYes
! Description: Replaces CookieYes's loader with their own reject-all, and
!              answers the IAB layer their file only stubs.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! ONE FILE IS THE WHOLE INSTALL. client_data/<id>/script.js carries the IAB TCF
! stub, their auto-blocker, the tenant's configuration AND the request for
! banner.js - so replacing it means banner.js is never asked for either, and
! neither is the 917KB global vendor list they ship. The second rule is for a
! page that references banner.js directly.
!
! Swap in cookieyes-reject-unblock.js on a site that withholds something until
! a category is on: same stored refusal, and the tags their plugin parked in
! the markup are let go.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||cdn-cookieyes.com/client_data/*/script.js$script,redirect=cookieyes-reject.js
||cdn-cookieyes.com/client_data/*/banner.js$script,redirect=cookieyes-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! THEIR RECORD is one cookie of comma-separated pairs, measured on a first
! visit to a tenant's own page:
!
!   cookieyes-consent=consentid:c3pOWHhYcHNXT3Byb2ljcTFtazJSeUFNZlZVcGV3Y2c,
!                     consent:,action:,necessary:,functional:,analytics:,
!                     performance:,advertisement:,other:
!
! Six categories, each yes or no, with consent the decision and action whether
! the visitor answered. The refusal written here is necessary yes and the other
! five no, consent no, action yes.
!
! THE consentid IS NOT MINTED. Theirs is 22 random characters; one already in
! the cookie is kept and otherwise it stays empty, because an id invented by a
! resource is an id the resource created and then handed to a page's analytics.
!
! THEIR EVENTS GO TO THE DOCUMENT, through one dispatcher that builds a
! CustomEvent: cookieyes_banner_load carries getCkyConsent(), and
! cookieyes_consent_update carries { accepted: [], rejected: [] }.
!
! IT IS DOMAIN-LOCKED, worth knowing before testing one: the registered domain
! is inlined in the file and compared to location.hostname by suffix, and a
! mismatch throws "Looks like your website URL has changed". Nothing in the
! resource checks a domain.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Deliberately left alone:
!
!   common/iab-gvl-v3.json   the global vendor list they ship, 917KB. With the
!                            loader replaced nothing asks for it, so there is
!                            nothing to match - and the version it declares,
!                            179 with policy version 5, is what the IAB layer
!                            here reports.
!   their blocker            it lives in the file being replaced: script.js
!                            patches document.createElement so a script whose
!                            src matches their provider list has its type
!                            flipped to javascript/blocked. uBlock Origin is
!                            what blocks once it is gone, and what their plugin
!                            parked in the markup stays parked.
!   their cookie purge       theirs deletes the cookies it lists per category,
!                            but that list is per tenant and inlined in the
!                            replaced file.
!
! To see it work:
!
!   [consent-rr] cookieyes-reject 1.0.0 categories=necessary cookie=written
!                consentid=none freed=0 events=2 tcf=refused banner=none
!                log=none
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
