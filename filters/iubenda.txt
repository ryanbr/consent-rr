! Title: consent-rr - iubenda
! Description: Replaces iubenda's Cookie Solution loader with a stub that
!              records their own refusal, answers their api, and leaves their
!              auto-blocker's work alone.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! What a page actually loads is a 4KB loader. It reads _iub.csConfiguration,
! installs window._cmp.pubSub, sets a few _iub fields and fetches the real
! thing - 450KB of it:
!
!   "https://cdn.iubenda.com/cookie_solution/iubenda_cs/1.108.0/core-"
!       + o.lang + ".js"
!
! Replace the loader and neither arrives.
!
! Swap in iubenda-accept.js where a refusal is not an option: theirs is often
! run as a consent wall, and a wall answers to the record rather than to the
! tags - freeing the parked scripts does not take the wall down.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||cdn.iubenda.com/cs/iubenda_cs.js$script,redirect=iubenda-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Their safemode loader, which is the same entry point with the core named by
! hash instead of version:
!
!   cdn.iubenda.com/cookie_solution/safemode/iubenda_cs.js
!   -> cookie_solution/iubenda_cs/core-<32 hex>.js
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||cdn.iubenda.com/cookie_solution/safemode/iubenda_cs.js$script,redirect=iubenda-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! The core itself, in both url shapes. With the loader replaced neither is
! requested, so these are belt and braces for a page that references one
! directly.
!
!   cookie_solution/iubenda_cs/1.108.0/core-en.js
!   cookie_solution/iubenda_cs/core-fcf8c9eac36aece9d290934b54a63296.js
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||cdn.iubenda.com/cookie_solution/iubenda_cs/*/core-*.js$script,redirect=noopjs
||cdn.iubenda.com/cookie_solution/iubenda_cs/core-*.js$script,redirect=noopjs

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! THEIR IAB STUBS GO, because this resource is the CMP now.
!
! A page loads these itself, ahead of the loader, and each one installs a
! window-level api that queues every call until their core arrives:
!
!   cs/tcf/stub-v2.js    __tcfapi, which answers ping with cmpStatus "stub"
!                        and pushes everything else into a closure array
!   cs/gpp/stub.js       __gpp, the same shape
!
! Left in place, a stub that loads AFTER this resource overwrites the working
! __tcfapi with one that answers nothing, and a vendor waiting on it waits for
! ever. Their full TCF module is named here for the same reason.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||cdn.iubenda.com/cs/tcf/stub-v2.js$script,redirect=noopjs
||cdn.iubenda.com/cs/tcf/safe-tcf-v2.js$script,redirect=noopjs
||cdn.iubenda.com/cs/gpp/stub.js$script,redirect=noopjs

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! DO NOT BLOCK THEIR AUTO-BLOCKER.
!
!   cs.iubenda.com/autoblocking/<siteId>.js
!
! That file is the thing parking the trackers: it rewrites a tag's src into
! data-suppressedsrc and marks it _iub_cs_activate before anything runs. Block
! it and every tracker on the page loads unparked, which is the opposite of
! what any of this is for. It is deliberately left alone, and so is their
! per-site configuration at
!
!   cs.iubenda.com/cookie-solution/confs/js/<cookiePolicyId>.js
!
! which only their core asks for.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! For a site that withholds content until consent.
!
! Same refusal stored and sent - the record is a refusal, consent mode is told
! denied and the IAB string grants nothing - while the page's own scripts are
! told every purpose is on and the tags their blocker parked are freed:
!
! example.com##+js(iubenda-reject-unblock)
!
! Where the site needs the consent itself rather than the tags, which is what a
! wall needs:
!
! example.com##+js(iubenda-accept)
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Where a redirect cannot land.
!
! A user resource is served as a data: URI, so a site whose CSP omits data: for
! scripts refuses it - and their loader is the one file that must run, because
! with it gone their auto-blocker's parked tags have nobody to free them and
! nothing answers their api. The scriptlet form is injected rather than
! fetched:
!
! example.com##+js(iubenda-reject)
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! THIS WAITS FOR THEIR CONFIGURATION, which is what makes the scriptlet form
! above safe. Their loader reads _iub.csConfiguration synchronously and cannot
! work without it, so a page always sets it above their script tag - where a
! redirect lands. A scriptlet runs earlier than that, at document_start,
! before the page has run anything, and this used to read an empty object:
! measured, that wrote a record named _iub_cs- with no tenant id, in their
! simple form on a per-purpose tenant, and fired none of the page's callbacks.
!
! Nothing is written until it exists. An empty configuration object is not the
! same thing as none: a settings object of theirs that is missing a field is a
! real deployment and still gets a record.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! THEIR RECORD, which is what stops the banner coming back.
!
! Their own getConsentObj builds it, and it is written where theirs is - a
! cookie named _iub_cs- plus their storage id, which is "s" + siteId when
! storage.useSiteId is on and the cookiePolicyId otherwise, URL-encoded
! because their own converter encodes a value that looks like JSON for a
! cookie and leaves it decoded for localStorage:
!
!   {"timestamp":"2026-10-06T00:00:00.000Z","version":"1.108.0",
!    "purposes":{"1":true,"2":false,"3":false,"4":false,"5":false}}
!
! Their five purposes, from their own per_purpose table: 1 necessary,
! 2 functionality, 3 experience, 4 measurement, 5 marketing. Purpose 1 is true
! in every mode because their own storeConsent forces it. A tenant without
! perPurposeConsent gets their simple form instead, "consent": false.
!
! THE PREFERENCE ID IS NOT MINTED: their cons.rand comes from their
! consent-log server, and a record without it simply has no preference id,
! which is a shape their own getPreferenceId handles. An existing one is
! carried over rather than replaced.
!
! To see it work:
!
!   [consent-rr] iubenda-reject 1.0.0 cookie=_iub_cs-7654321 stored=cookie
!                mode=per-purpose accepted=necessary surface=denied
!                tcf=refused told=2 banner=none sent=none
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! WHAT THIS DOES NOT ANSWER YET.
!
!   __gpp and usprivacy       their US flow writes _iub_cs-uspr and usprivacy
!                             and answers __gpp through a module of its own.
!                             Nothing here writes or answers either, so a US
!                             page gets the refusal above and no US signal.
!   their CCPA opt-out        isCcpaAcknowledged and isCcpaOptedOut answer
!                             false, which is their own default for a visitor
!                             who was never asked.
!   Global Privacy Control    their own getPreferences applies a GPC signal to
!                             the US purposes s, sh and adv. A refusal here is
!                             already stronger than that, and accept is an
!                             explicit choice that overrides it.
!   an additional-consent     the granting one would need the tenant's own
!   string for accept         Google vendor list, which is not on the page.
!                             The refusal carries 2~~dv, the shape their
!                             gacVersion 2 gives for consenting to nothing.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
