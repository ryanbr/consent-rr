! Title: consent-rr - CookieConsent (Orest Bida)
! Description: Replaces CookieConsent v3 with a stub that records their own
!              refusal, answers their whole api and leaves their parked script
!              tags parked.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! CookieConsent v3 by Orest Bida - the MIT-licensed one, NOT Osano's old
! library of the same common name. Osano's current CMP has its own list here.
!
! One rule, because the file is named the same wherever it comes from, and
! every one of these is a real deployment:
!
!   cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/orestbida/cookieconsent@3.1.0/dist/cookieconsent.umd.js
!   cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/orestbida/cookieconsent@v3.0.0/dist/...
!   cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/orestbida/cookieconsent@v3.0.0-rc.17/dist/...
!   cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/vanilla-cookieconsent@3/dist/...     their npm name
!   unpkg.com/vanilla-cookieconsent@3.1.0/dist/...
!   www.csc.edu/media/website/site-assets/javascript/cookieconsent.umd.js
!
! The last one is self-hosted, and it is byte-identical to the 3.1.0 file on
! jsDelivr - same sha1 - which is why one resource stands in for all of them.
!
! Swap in cookieconsent-accept.js where a refusal is not an option.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
/cookieconsent.umd.js$script,redirect=cookieconsent-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! For a site that withholds something until a category is on.
!
! Same stored refusal, while the page's own scripts are told every category is
! accepted and the tags their manager parked are freed by their own contract:
!
! example.com##+js(cookieconsent-reject-unblock)
!
! Where the site needs the consent itself:
!
! example.com##+js(cookieconsent-accept)
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! THEIR MODULE BUILD IS LEFT ALONE.
!
!   cookieconsent.esm.js
!
! A page that takes that one imports it and calls run() off the import, so a
! stub that sets window.CookieConsent answers nobody - and a bundler's copy,
! compiled into a site's own application file, cannot be reached at all. On
! either of those the banner stays.
!
! Their stylesheet is left alone too: with no banner built, cookieconsent.css
! styles nothing, and blocking it would be one more request for no reason.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! THEIR RECORD, which is what stops the banner coming back. Their own gate:
!
!   e.V && _ && d !== t.revision && (e.j = !1)
!   e.T = !(_ && e.j && e.C && e.S && f)
!
! a string consentId, a revision equal to the page's, a consentTimestamp, a
! lastConsentTimestamp and categories as an array. Miss any one and their
! banner is back, so all of them are written:
!
!   {"categories":["necessary"],"revision":0,"data":null,
!    "consentTimestamp":"...","consentId":"<uuid>",
!    "services":{"necessary":[]},"languageCode":"en",
!    "lastConsentTimestamp":"...","expirationTime":<ms>}
!
! THE consentId IS MINTED HERE, which is the opposite of what this repo does
! for the families whose id comes from a consent-log server. Theirs is made in
! the browser, with crypto.getRandomValues, so this makes it the same way - and
! an existing one is carried over rather than replaced, along with their
! consentTimestamp.
!
! It goes where theirs goes: a cookie named by their cookie.name, cc_cookie by
! default, as URL-encoded JSON with their own attributes - or localStorage
! where their cookie.useLocalStorage says so.
!
! To see it work:
!
!   [consent-rr] cookieconsent-reject 1.0.0 store=cookie name=cc_cookie
!                accepted=necessary type=necessary surface=necessary parked=3
!                freed=0 cleared=4 told=4 banner=none
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! THEIR AUTO-CLEAR IS DONE, because it is the visible half of a refusal. A
! category with autoClear.cookies has those cookies deleted their own way -
! host-only, the configured domain dot-prefixed, and for a www host the bare
! domain as well - and an entry written as a regexp is matched against the
! cookies actually there, as theirs is.
!
! THEIR reloadPage IS NOT. Theirs reloads the page when a category goes from
! accepted to refused, and a resource that writes a refusal on every load
! would reload every load.
!
! BOTS ARE LEFT ALONE, because theirs leaves them alone: with their
! hideFromBots on, which is their default, a user agent matching
! /bot|crawl|spider|slurp|teoma/i or navigator.webdriver makes their own run()
! return before doing anything, so this returns too.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
