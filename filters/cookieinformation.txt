! Title: consent-rr - Cookie Information
! Description: Replaces Cookie Information's uc.js with a stub that reports an
!              already-made decision, instead of waiting for a banner.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.
!
! Host-anchored, unlike the OneTrust list. That one matches on filename because
! the SDK is served from several CDNs under three different path layouts; here
! the host is distinctive and the filename is "uc.js", which is far too generic
! to match on its own.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! The necessary category only, everything else denied. One resource: no
! accept-all or unblock variant for this consent manager.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||policy.app.cookieinformation.com/uc.js$script,redirect=cookieinformation-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Sites whose CSP omits data: for scripts.
!
! A user resource has no extension URL, so uBO serves it as a data: URI, and a
! script-src-elem or default-src without data: refuses it. Nothing of ours runs -
! no cookie, no console line, CookieInformation.consentRR undefined - while the
! banner still disappears, because the redirect above kept the real SDK out. That
! reads as success with a missing cookie, which is how it was first reported.
!
! The scriptlet form is injected rather than fetched, so the directive does not
! apply. The redirect above still does its half of the job: keeping the real SDK
! out. Both lines are needed on these sites.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
almbrand.dk##+js(cookieinformation-reject)


! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! uc.js is what asks for these, so with it replaced they are never requested.
! They only matter where something unblocks uc.js itself: the per-site dialog
! text and configuration, the cross-domain consent-sharing frame, and the cookie
! table the banner renders.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||policy.app.cookieinformation.com/latest/*$script,redirect=noopjs
||policy.app.cookieinformation.com/cookiesharingiframe.html$subdocument,redirect=noopframe
||policy.app.cookieinformation.com/cookie-data/*$xhr,redirect=noopjson
