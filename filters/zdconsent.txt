! Title: consent-rr - Ziff Davis zdconsent
! Description: Replaces Ziff Davis's own consent layer with a stub that
!              records their refusal, runs the queues a page waits on, and
!              never asks for OneTrust.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! TWO FILES, ONE DIFFERENCE. zdconsent_eu.js is byte-identical to zdconsent.js
! but for two booleans - gdprApplies and optinApplies - baked true, and the
! site decides which one a visitor is served. One wildcard covers both, and
! whichever was asked for, the resource reads it back off the script element:
! uBO redirects the request, so the element keeps its original src.
!
! Swap in zdconsent-accept.js on a site that needs consent to be usable - see
! the README, it is the narrow case.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||cdn.ziffstatic.com/jst/zdconsent*.js$script,redirect=zdconsent-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! IT IS A ONETRUST FRONT END, which is why there is nothing else to add here.
! Their script's first act is to inject OneTrust's otSDKStub.js from
! cdn.cookielaw.org, and the function that decides what is consented reads
! OneTrust's own record - the OptanonConsent cookie's groups, or
! window.OnetrustActiveGroups - over the top of its own defaults.
!
! So the resource is the OneTrust layer plus theirs, and with their script
! replaced nothing asks cdn.cookielaw.org for anything. A site that also loads
! OneTrust directly is covered by filters/onetrust.txt; the two stubs see each
! other and the second one stands down.
!
! THE QUEUES ARE THE POINT. A page pushes work into window.zdconsent.run /
! cmd / analytics / functional / social / useractioncomplete, and their script
! runs each queue when the matching consent exists. run is ungated - mashable.com
! queues nine things into it, speedtest.net declares the queues inline above the
! tag - so blocking the file outright leaves all of it unrun. The stub runs run
! and useractioncomplete whatever was decided, and leaves the consent-gated
! ones parked exactly as their own Deny All does.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Deliberately left alone:
!
!   cdn.ziffstatic.com/pg/*.js   their ad stack. On speedtest.net the inline
!                               onload AND onerror both resolve the page's
!                               __pogoReady promise, so blocking it strands
!                               nothing.
!   the adblock report          their script sets window.adblock, calls
!                               Pogo.setADB(true), writes a _pgabp cookie and
!                               sends gtag('event', 'adblock'). The stub does
!                               none of it.
!   https://zdbb.net/optout     their own refusal calls it server-side. A
!                               redirected script talks to nobody.
!
! To see it work, the two console lines name each layer and what it wrote:
!
!   [consent-rr] zdconsent-reject/onetrust 1.0.0 groups=,C0001, tcf=refused
!                gpp=refused
!   [consent-rr] zdconsent-reject 1.0.0 variant=default gdpr=false geo=unknown
!                consent=refused gpc=unset usp=1YYY cookies=4 queues=2 events=3
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
