! Title: consent-rr - Usercentrics
! Description: Replaces Usercentrics' loader.js with a stub that records a
!              refusal in the record their own blocker reads.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Usercentrics ships in two halves and only one of them is replaced here.
!
! loader.js is the CMP: it reads the configuration off its own script tag,
! fetches the SDK, the controller and the banner - around 450kB across four
! files - and renders the dialog. That is what this stands in for, so none of
! it is requested.
!
! The regional host varies by tenant (eu1, us1 and the rest), which the leading
! || covers.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||cmp.usercentrics.eu/ui/loader.js$script,redirect=usercentrics-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Leave uc-block.bundle.js alone.
!
! That is the other half: their blocker, served from
! privacy-proxy.usercentrics.eu, and it is the thing that enforces a refusal
! rather than the CMP. It carries a list of some 111 providers with the
! patterns that match their scripts, iframes, images and embeds, and its own
! rule is
!
!   disabled = every provider NOT in the whitelist
!
! where the whitelist holds the service ids that have consent. It builds that
! whitelist out of localStorage, not out of the CMP, so with the CMP replaced
! by a refusal it blocks everything - 89 script patterns and 86 iframe patterns
! in the bundle sampled. Blocking or nooping this file would switch that off
! and leave uBlock Origin to do all of it alone, so it is deliberately kept.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Where a redirect cannot land.
!
! A user resource is served as a data: URI, so a site whose CSP omits data: for
! scripts refuses it - nothing of ours runs while the banner is gone anyway,
! because the redirect kept the loader out. The scriptlet form is injected
! rather than fetched, so the directive does not apply to it.
!
! The same line covers a tenant serving the CMP through their privacy proxy on
! its own domain, where there is no usercentrics.eu url to match:
!
! example.com##+js(usercentrics-reject)
!
! Injected that way it runs before the blocker rather than after, which the
! resource handles - the blocker then reads the refusal out of storage at
! construction instead of being told about it.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! The loader is what asks for these, so with it replaced they are never
! requested. They only matter where something else loads them: the SDK, the
! legislation-specific controller, the banner views and their stylesheet.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||cmp.usercentrics.eu/ui/v/*/WebSdk.lib.*.js$script,redirect=noopjs
||cmp.usercentrics.eu/ui/*CmpController.*.js$script,redirect=noopjs
||cmp.usercentrics.eu/ui/*CmpView.*.js$script,redirect=noopjs
||cmp.usercentrics.eu/ui/*.css$stylesheet,redirect=noopcss
