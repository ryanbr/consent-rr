! Title: consent-rr - Google Funding Choices
! Description: Replaces Funding Choices with the inactive path Google's own
!              script takes, and clears the consent cookie their readers read.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! BLOCKING THE REQUEST IS ALREADY SAFE, and this does not change that. It is
! here for what blocking leaves behind: a page watching for the iframes their
! script appends waits for something that is never coming, and a visitor who
! consented before installing a blocker keeps the cookie that says so.
!
! One host rule covers every shape they serve a script from - /i/pub-<id>,
! /f/<token>, /l/<token> - because the token is minted per page and there is
! nothing stable in the path to match.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||fundingchoicesmessages.google.com^$script,redirect=fundingchoices-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! WHAT THE RESOURCE REPRODUCES is their /f/<token> script, which is what their
! loader serves when Funding Choices has decided it has nothing to show. All of
! it was measured by booting that script, not read from a doc:
!
!   two hidden iframes in the TOP document, named googlefcInactive and
!   googlefcLoaded. The googlefcPresent one is the publisher's own inline
!   snippet's and is left alone.
!
!   window.googlefc.__fci as a queue that ANSWERS instead of collecting, over
!   exactly two command names - "loaded" and "prov" - each answered with the
!   string [null,[4]], and anything else with [null,[2]].
!
!   window.__fcInternalApiManager and window.__fcInternalApiPostMessageReady,
!   with a listener that answers {__fciCall: {command, callId}} with
!   {__fciReturn: "[<callId>,[4]]"}. A command outside the two, or a call with
!   no numeric callId, gets silence - which is also theirs.
!
! THE REFUSAL IS THEIR COOKIE'S ABSENCE. gpt.js and adsbygoogle.js each read
! exactly one Funding Choices thing, the FCCDCF cookie: URI-decoded if it
! starts with %, parsed as JSPB, field 4, whose field 1 is the TC string. No
! cookie means no TC string. Writing one would mint a consent string, so
! nothing is written - and an FCCDCF a visitor consented to earlier is cleared,
! on the host and on the registrable domain, because theirs is set for about
! thirteen months.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Deliberately left alone:
!
!   googlefc.callbackQueue   their own inactive path does not run it - pushing
!                            two callbacks before booting their script leaves
!                            both unrun and the queue two long - so neither
!                            does this. The script that drains them is the
!                            messaging one, served from a token-bound url that
!                            answers 403 to anything but the page it was minted
!                            for, so its key names cannot be measured either.
!
!   __tcfapi                 their messaging script is a TCF CMP; this is not.
!                            Nothing provides one while the request is blocked,
!                            which is the state being replaced, and a useful
!                            one would have to invent a TC string.
!
!   anything on the host     that is not a script. The rule is $script, so a
!                            beacon or a frame stays with whatever blocks it
!                            today.
!
! To see it work:
!
!   [consent-rr] fundingchoices-reject 1.0.0 frames=2 answered=0 replied=0
!                cleared=1 tcf=absent
!
! cleared=1 is a visitor who had consented before this was installed.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
