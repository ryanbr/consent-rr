! Title: consent-rr - Google Funding Choices
! Description: Replaces Funding Choices with the inactive path Google's own
!              script takes, and clears the consent cookie their readers read.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! BLOCKING THE REQUEST IS ALREADY SAFE, and this does not change that. It is
! here for what blocking leaves behind: a page watching for the iframes their
! script appends waits for something that is never coming, and a visitor who
! consented before installing a blocker keeps the cookie that says so.
!
! One host rule covers every shape they serve a script from - /i/pub-<id>,
! /f/<token>, /l/<token> - because the token is minted per page and there is
! nothing stable in the path to match.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||fundingchoicesmessages.google.com^$script,redirect=fundingchoices-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! WHAT THE RESOURCE REPRODUCES is their /f/<token> script, which is what their
! loader serves when Funding Choices has decided it has nothing to show. All of
! it was measured by booting that script, not read from a doc:
!
!   two hidden iframes in the TOP document, named googlefcInactive and
!   googlefcLoaded. The googlefcPresent one is the publisher's own inline
!   snippet's and is left alone.
!
!   window.googlefc.__fci as a queue that ANSWERS instead of collecting, over
!   exactly two command names - "loaded" and "prov" - each answered with the
!   string [null,[4]], and anything else with [null,[2]].
!
!   window.__fcInternalApiManager and window.__fcInternalApiPostMessageReady,
!   with a listener that answers {__fciCall: {command, callId}} with
!   {__fciReturn: "[<callId>,[4]]"}. A command outside the two, or a call with
!   no numeric callId, gets silence - which is also theirs.
!
! THE IAB LAYER IS ANSWERED, AND REFUSES. Their messaging script is the TCF
! CMP, and it is the one thing this inactive path does not put up - so a page
! or a vendor waiting on __tcfapi waits forever once the request is blocked.
! __tcfapi answers here as cmpId 300, which is Google LLC on the IAB's own
! published list, with gdprApplies true and a string that grants nothing in
! any set it carries - purposes, legitimate interests, vendors, special
! features, publisher. The locator frame and the postMessage relay go up with
! it, so a vendor in a frame is answered too. Nothing is stored: no
! euconsent-v2 and no IABTCF_ key, because theirs keeps the string in the
! cookie below, which is the one being refused.
!
! __uspapi ANSWERS TOO, and it is the one their own US flow would have put up.
! Both of Google's readers ask for it - gpt.js and adsbygoogle.js each call
! getUSPData and read uspString - and each falls back to 1--- when nothing
! answers, which is "not applicable" rather than an opt-out. The string here
! is 1-Y- where the browser sends Global Privacy Control and 1--- otherwise,
! the same answer osano-reject.js and inmobi-reject.js give: whether CCPA
! applies at all comes from a location lookup a page does not have, so the
! notice and LSPA characters stay dashes rather than claim something about
! the publisher, and the middle one is the visitor's own signal. Brave sends
! it by default. Their one command is getUSPData and anything else gets
! silence; a version that is given and is not 1 is answered (null, false),
! while a call that sends none is answered anyway - gpt.js' own stand-in
! checks the version not at all, and refusing such a call would deliver
! neither the refusal nor the end of the wait. Where that stand-in got in
! first it is replaced, because it carries window.__uspapiManager set to
! "fc" and serves a string read out of the cookie being cleared; a CMP of
! the publisher's own keeps its API.
!
! THE PAGE SIDE IS ANSWERED, because that is what a site gates its content on.
! Everything a publisher's wrapper reads, it reads inside a googlefc callback,
! so a wrapper that puts up a loader and waits never proceeds while the queue
! only collects. The queue here runs them, over the four keys theirs releases
! with the getters this resource has - consentData and legacyNoData, then
! adBlockData, then consentApi - and on a wait for the body, because a callback
! released at document_start is one doing DOM work against nothing. The other
! four stay parked: initialCcpaData, initialUsStatesData,
! initialUsStatesOptOutData and consentModeData come with getters this resource
! does not have, and releasing a callback into a getter that is not there
! trades a wait for a TypeError.
!
! getAdBlockerStatus, getWhitelistStatus and getAllowAdsStatus answer UNKNOWN,
! which is their own value before detection. A wall is written as "if the
! status is EXTENSION_LEVEL_AD_BLOCKER, or NOT_WHITELISTED, or ADS_NOT_ALLOWED,
! put it up", and UNKNOWN passes that without claiming anything about the
! visitor. NO_AD_BLOCKER, WHITELISTED and ADS_ALLOWED are not on offer: that is
! not privacy, it is defeating ad-block detection, which is a different job and
! belongs in a list rather than in a consent resource. getConsentStatus is
! NO_CONSENT, where theirs answers UNKNOWN always, because a refusal is what
! happened and a page polling for a settled status would spin on UNKNOWN.
! getConsentedProviderIds is empty, showRevocationMessage does nothing, and
! their five enums go up by their own values. Nothing is detected and nothing
! is stored.
!
! THE REFUSAL IS THEIR COOKIE'S ABSENCE. gpt.js and adsbygoogle.js each read
! exactly one Funding Choices thing, the FCCDCF cookie: URI-decoded if it
! starts with %, parsed as JSPB, field 4, whose field 1 is the TC string. No
! cookie means no TC string. Writing one would mint a consent string, so
! nothing is written - and an FCCDCF a visitor consented to earlier is cleared,
! on the host and on the registrable domain, because theirs is set for about
! thirteen months.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Deliberately left alone:
!
!   __gpp                    their script carries one, fourteen references to
!                            __gpp and two to __gppLocator, but neither
!                            reader asks for it: gpt.js and adsbygoogle.js
!                            mention it zero times each, so nothing is left
!                            waiting on it. It goes in when something that
!                            waits on it turns up.
!
!   anything on the host     that is not a script. The rule is $script, so a
!                            beacon or a frame stays with whatever blocks it
!                            today.
!
! To see it work:
!
!   [consent-rr] fundingchoices-reject 1.1.6 frames=2 answered=0 replied=0
!                cleared=1 tcf=refused usp=1--- queued=0
!
! cleared=1 is a visitor who had consented before this was installed. usp is
! the string that went up - 1-Y- on a browser sending Global Privacy Control,
! or "theirs" where a publisher's own US flow was already standing. queued is
! how many callbacks a publisher had parked before the request was blocked,
! and "theirs" where a queue of their own was already standing.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
