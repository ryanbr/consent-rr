! Title: consent-rr - Sourcepoint
! Description: Replaces Sourcepoint's unified wrapper with a stored refusal:
!              no message iframe, their _sp_ surface and event callbacks
!              answering, and __tcfapi refusing as cmpId 6.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! ONE FILE IS THE WHOLE CMP. wrapperMessagingWithoutDetection.js is a webpack
! bundle whose campaign modules are chunks it fetches itself, from its own
! publicPath:
!
!   window._sp_.config.baseEndpoint || "https://cdn.privacy-mgmt.com"
!       + "/unified/4.40.3/"
!
! ccpa, ccpa-gpp, custom, gdpr, gdpr-tcf, globalcmp, hbbtv, preferences, usnat
! and usnat-uspapi. Nothing else ever asks for them, so replacing the wrapper
! takes the message iframe, the privacy manager and the consent call with it -
! and there is no banner left to hide, because their banner is one of those
! iframes.
!
! Matched by filename rather than by host and path: the same file is served
! from cdn.privacy-mgmt.com, from their preprod endpoint, from a versioned
! /unified/<version>/ path when a page asks for one with _sp_version, and from
! a first-party host where a tenant points their own CDN at it.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
/wrapperMessagingWithoutDetection.js$script,important,redirect=sourcepoint-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! WHY important. Blocking this file is what the lists already do in places, and
! it breaks the sites that wall off behind it - which is why there are so many
! exceptions for it in the big lists:
!
!   @@||privacy-mgmt.com^$script,domain=n-tv.de
!   @@||privacy-mgmt.com^$script,domain=chip.de
!   @@||privacy-mgmt.com^$script,domain=bild.de
!   @@||privacy-mgmt.com^$script,domain=handelsblatt.com
!   @@||privacy-mgmt.com^$script,domain=faz.net|focus.de|golem.de|stern.de
!   @@||cdn.privacy-mgmt.com/wrapperMessagingWithoutDetection.js$script,domain=giga.de|spieletipps.de
!
! An exception beats a plain $redirect, so without important the resource never
! loads on exactly the sites it was written for. Those exceptions exist because
! the page needs an answer, not because it needs their banner - and an answer
! is what this gives it. A site-wide allowlist of the user's own still wins,
! which is the behaviour to keep.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! WHAT THE RESOURCE REPRODUCES is the state their own script reaches on a
! property with nothing to show. All of it measured in build 4.40.3 of that
! file and in its gdpr-tcf chunk:
!
!   window._sp_, with their own surface on it - addEventListener,
!   removeEventListener, destroyMessages, destroyMessaging, resetUserState,
!   requestUUID, version, writeCookie, metricUrl, metricData, gcm,
!   updateGoogleConsentMode, and the per-campaign objects ccpa, gdpr,
!   globalcmp, preferences and usnat.
!
!   window._sp_queue handed over the way theirs hands it over: what is queued
!   runs with the _sp_ object, the array is kept as _sp_.processedQueue, and
!   _sp_queue is replaced by one whose push IS the runner, so a later push
!   executes at once.
!
!   _sp_.gdpr with deleteConsentCookies, loadPrivacyManagerModal,
!   loadPrivacyManagerOtt, loadNativeOtt, getCustomVendorConsents,
!   postCustomConsent, postRejectAll and getVendorPurposeMapping. The privacy
!   manager resolves without a dialog - theirs is an iframe from a chunk that
!   is never fetched - and getVendorPurposeMapping answers (null, false),
!   which is their own failure path for it.
!
!   their event callbacks, in their order: onSPPMObjectReady, onSPReady,
!   onMessageReceiveData with {messageId: 0}, onConsentReady, then
!   sp:onBeforeShowMessages at the window with messageCount 0,
!   onInteractionComplete and sp:onAfterShowMessages.
!
! THEIR CAMPAIGNS DO NOT ANSWER ALIKE, which is worth reading off their own
! chunks rather than assuming. Each one's checkForMessage, with nothing to
! show:
!
!   gdpr          onConsentReady [uuid, euconsent,
!                 {addtlConsent, applies, consentedToAll}]
!   ccpa          onConsentReady [uuid, uspString, {applies, gpcEnabled}]
!   usnat         the same two fields as ccpa
!   globalcmp     the same again
!   custom        onMessageReceiveData and nothing else
!   preferences   onMessageReceiveData and nothing else
!
! so a US campaign is answered in their US shape - a page reading
! info.gpcEnabled gets it, read the way their own wrapper reads it, and one
! reading consentedToAll does not get a field their ccpa path never carries.
! The US string is null: theirs is minted by their consent service, and
! __uspapi and __gpp are not answered here.
!
! THEIR CALLBACK SIGNATURE PUTS THE CAMPAIGN FIRST, which is worth knowing if
! you read their docs instead of their dispatcher: executeEventCallback calls
! each registered function as cb.call(null, category, ...args), so a page's
! onConsentReady is called (campaign, uuid, euconsent, info).
!
! THE IAB LAYER IS ANSWERED, AND REFUSES. "__tcfapi" does not appear in the
! wrapper at all - it is in the gdpr-tcf chunk - so blocking the file leaves
! every vendor waiting on a CMP that is never coming. __tcfapi answers here as
! cmpId 6, which is their own default in the bundle and Sourcepoint
! Technologies, Inc. on the IAB's published list, with cmpVersion 1, policy
! version 5, apiVersion "2" and a string that grants nothing in any set it
! carries. Their five custom commands answer too. The postMessage relay goes
! up only where the page's own IAB stub had not already defined __tcfapi,
! because that stub's relay forwards to this one and two replies to one
! callId is what answering as well would mean - and the locator frame only
! where there is none. The stub is what that is decided on rather than the
! frame: a snippet that adds a frame and no relay would otherwise leave every
! vendor in a frame unanswered.
!
! THE REFUSAL IS euconsent-v2 AND NOTHING ELSE OF THEIRS. Their own script
! writes no consent cookie directly: the campaign response carries a cookies
! array and their setCookiesAction writes what their service says, so
! consentUUID and the rest are their server's to mint. euconsent-v2 is the one
! in that set anything else reads, so that one is written, with the refusal.
! A record a visitor consented to before this was installed is cleared - the
! localStorage record under _sp_user_consent_<propertyId>, its
! _sp_sync_consent_ twin, the legacy _sp_user_consent their own cleanOldConsent
! removes, and the consentUUID that keys them.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Deliberately left alone:
!
!   wrapperMessagingWithDetection.js   their adblock-detection wrapper, which
!                                      is a different file and was not
!                                      measured. Replacing it would also
!                                      replace the detection a page may act
!                                      on, so it keeps whatever handles it
!                                      today.
!
!   gdprApplies                        a cookie of theirs that says whether
!                                      the regulation applies to this
!                                      visitor, which is not an answer a
!                                      visitor gave. Neither written nor
!                                      cleared.
!
!   __gpp and __uspapi                 a CCPA, usnat or globalcmp property
!                                      gets the _sp_ surface and no US
!                                      signal: those live in three more
!                                      chunks, and their refusal strings are
!                                      minted by their consent service rather
!                                      than being derivable from the page.
!
!   their Google consent mode          _sp_.updateGoogleConsentMode takes a
!                                      callback and nothing is pushed to a
!                                      page's dataLayer, because the gcmStatus
!                                      their callbacks are handed is built by
!                                      their service.
!
!   everything but scripts             the rule is $script.
!
! On a CSP site, or on Chromium where a $redirect= to a user resource cannot
! work at all, inject it instead - same resource, no request to redirect:
!
! example.com##+js(sourcepoint-reject)
!
! To see it work:
!
!   [consent-rr] sourcepoint-reject 1.0.0 campaigns=gdpr tcf=refused written=1
!                cleared=0 events=4 queued=1 banner=none
!
! cleared=1 or more is a visitor who had answered before this was installed.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
