! Title: consent-rr - Transcend
! Description: Replaces Transcend's ui.js with a stub that records a refusal
!              through airgap's own API, instead of building a banner.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Replace the banner, not the engine.
!
! Transcend ships in two halves. airgap.js is the engine and carries the
! tenant's whole configuration - the purposes, the cookie-to-purpose table, the
! allowed hosts - and it blocks requests and cookies itself, by consent. ui.js
! is the banner, 390kB of Preact, which airgap loads only when it decides to
! prompt.
!
! So airgap.js is deliberately left alone: with a refusal recorded it is the
! thing enforcing it. Replacing it would drop that blocking and mean
! reimplementing the API it exposes.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||transcend-cdn.com/cm*/*/ui.js$script,redirect=transcend-reject.js
||transcend-cdn.com/cm*/*/uiV2.js$script,redirect=transcend-reject.js

! Two names and two paths, because their builds differ. An older one points at
! one UI for the whole tenant:
!
!   ui: "/cm/<id>/ui.js"
!
! and a newer one names it per regime, under a different path prefix:
!
!   [[...regions...], ["at-eu-uk-sui-can-quebec"], ..., [{"url":"uiV2.js","kind":"ui"}], ...]
!
! Airtable is the second kind, served from /cm-test/. The engine and the API are
! the same in both, so the resource is too - only the filename and the path
! prefix move.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Self-hosted banners.
!
! A tenant may serve the bundle from its own assets rather than the CDN, at a
! path of its own choosing - Mayo Clinic does, while its engine still comes from
! transcend-cdn.com - so no rule above can reach it and it needs naming:
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||assets.mayoclinic.org/content/dam/cpm-transcend/ui.js$script,redirect=transcend-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Where the banner cannot be found at all.
!
! A tenant that both self-hosts and names the file something of its own leaves
! nothing to match on. The scriptlet does not need to find it: it records the
! refusal through airgap before the banner is ever fetched.
!
! example.com##+js(transcend-reject)
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Served first-party.
!
! A tenant may serve the same files from its own host by CNAME - Costco uses
! consent.costco.ca - and those need naming per site:
!
! ||consent.costco.ca/cm*/*/ui.js$script,redirect=transcend-reject.js
! ||consent.costco.ca/cm*/*/uiV2.js$script,redirect=transcend-reject.js
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Sites with a consent UI of their own.
!
! A site may build its own banner on airgap rather than use ui.js - Costco's
! costco-consent-manager.iife.js does, and calls setConsent(null, ...) itself,
! which is how we know a tenant with auth off takes a decision that was not
! made by clicking.
!
! Such a banner reads the same refusal, so it has nothing to prompt for - but it
! may ask before airgap has got as far as loading ui.js. Injecting the scriptlet
! at document_start records the refusal before anything renders, and the
! redirect above then has nothing left to do:
!
! costco.ca##+js(transcend-reject)
! costco.com##+js(transcend-reject)
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! ON CHROMIUM, USE THE SCRIPTLET. A redirect= rule naming a user resource
! cannot work in Chrome, Brave or Edge: uBO can only serve one of yours as a
! data: URI, and Chromium refuses to redirect a request to data:. The field
! report was
!
!   GET https://transcend-cdn.com/cm/<id>/airgap.js net::ERR_UNSAFE_REDIRECT
!
! - the request fails outright, so neither their script nor the replacement
! loads. The scriptlet form does not redirect anything, and this family needs
! no block rule beside it: their engine has to load, and their ui.js is never
! fetched once a refusal is recorded.
!
!   example.com##+js(transcend-reject)
!
! And it takes no domain, so one line can cover every site a list would ever
! want it on:
!
!   *##+js(transcend-reject)
!
! which is safe because this resource waits for their own self.airgap to be
! defined before it does anything at all. On a page that never defines it
! there is no global carrying a value, no cookie, no storage and no line. No
! block rule is wanted beside it either: their engine has to load, and their
! ui.js is never fetched once a refusal is recorded.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! OR NAME THE SITES, because it only has to run once on each of them.
!
! Their record is persistent and their own boot takes it: with a confirmed
! refusal stored - in localStorage[tcmConsent], and in the tcm cookie where
! the tenant configures one - their engine does not prompt, and so never
! fetches the banner at all. The tenants read here carry
!
!   consentExpiry: 525600     minutes, so a year
!   onConsentExpiry: Prompt
!
! so one run answers for a year, and the line wants leaving in place for the
! next one - and for a visitor who clears their storage.
!
! The sites below are the Transcend tenants this repo has evidence for. uBO
! matches subdomains too, so costco.ca covers www.costco.ca:
!
airtable.com,costco.ca,costco.com,indiegogo.com,mayoclinic.org,transcend.io##+js(transcend-reject)
!
! To add one of your own: look for a request to transcend-cdn.com/cm*/ or for
! an airgap.js anywhere, then put that site's domain on the line. The console
! line says whether it took - via=setConsent or via=load on a page of theirs,
! and nothing at all on a page that is not.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! WHAT NOT TO WRITE, both reported from the field as "nothing happens, and no
! cookie is set":
!
!   ||transcend-cdn.com^$script,redirect-rule=transcend-reject.js
!
! Two separate faults in one line.
!
!   1. ^$script matches every script on their CDN, airgap.js included - so the
!      engine is the thing being redirected. On Chromium that request then
!      fails with ERR_UNSAFE_REDIRECT and airgap.js does not load at all; on
!      Firefox it loads the banner's stub in its place. Either way the engine
!      is gone. Nothing then records a refusal,
!      because this queues on airgap and airgap never arrives; and nothing
!      enforces one either, because airgap is the half that does the blocking.
!      Name the banner: the two rules at the top of this list do.
!
!   2. redirect-rule= only fires where some other filter blocks the request. On
!      its own it does nothing at all, and their real ui.js loads as usual. The
!      rules here use redirect=, which blocks and serves in one.
!
! Either way the console says so now, a second after load, and it tells that
! apart from their engine arriving and recording nothing:
!
!   [consent-rr] transcend-reject 1.3.1 refused=(none) via=no engine
!   [consent-rr] transcend-reject 1.3.1 refused=(none) via=no record
!
! and where their engine is in the page but never becomes ready, the purposes
! their own prelude carries are enough to write the refusal to their cookie for
! the next page:
!
!   [consent-rr] transcend-reject 1.3.1 refused=Functional,SaleOfInfo,
!                Advertising,Marketing,Sales,Analytics via=cookie+storage,
!                no engine
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! WHERE THEIR RECORD LIVES, and why there may be no tcm cookie to look at.
! Their persister writes two stores and the cookie is the conditional one:
!
!   mt[Hn] = ...            localStorage["tcmConsent"], always
!   if ( Zr ) { mu(i) }     the cookie, where Zr = C && qa && Wv
!
! with qa the site or the sites entry matching this host, and Wv requiring
! localSync to be neither private nor private-only - all three off their
! loadOptions. So a tenant that configures neither site nor a matching sites
! entry never writes a tcm cookie at all and keeps the record in
! localStorage.tcmConsent. Seeing tcmConsent and no cookie is the refusal
! recorded, not a failure. Their own log says which store it read:
!
!   Consent read from localStorage[tcmConsent]
!   Consent read from cookie (tcm)
!
! and airgap.loadOptions.site / .sites / .localSync say whether a cookie was
! ever going to be written. The fallback here writes both, or storage alone
! where localSync is private-only, because their cookie read is behind that
! same flag. A partitioned tenant gets their partitioned shapes:
! tcmMPConsent keyed by partition, and a tcm-<partition> cookie.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! READING THEIR COOKIE AFTERWARDS. A purpose may still say "Auto" in tcm once
! this has run, and that is not the refusal failing. Five genuine records off
! five tenants show it: GcmAdvanced on one, AlwaysBlock on another, Functional
! on a third - and Functional is configurable on other tenants, so which
! purpose it is is the tenant's choice rather than a class of purpose. Their
! own writer applies a value only where the purpose is configurable, so one
! their tenant does not hold configurable keeps whatever its default resolved
! to. Every purpose is asked to go off; theirs decides which of them move.
!
! confirmed:true is the field their engine gates the prompt on. updated is not
! a gate and is not constant in their own records either - four of those five
! say true, one false.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! ui.js is what asks for these, so with the banner replaced they are not
! fetched. The cross-domain frame is what syncs a decision between a tenant's
! domains; with a refusal recorded, a refusal is what it syncs.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||transcend-cdn.com/cm*/*/metadata.json$xhr,redirect=noopjson
||transcend-cdn.com/cm*/*/uiV2/themes/*.css$stylesheet,redirect=noopcss
||transcend-cdn.com/cm*/*/uiV2/translations/*$xhr,redirect=noopjson
