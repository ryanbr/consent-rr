! Title: consent-rr - Transcend
! Description: Replaces Transcend's ui.js with a stub that records a refusal
!              through airgap's own API, instead of building a banner.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Replace the banner, not the engine.
!
! Transcend ships in two halves. airgap.js is the engine and carries the
! tenant's whole configuration - the purposes, the cookie-to-purpose table, the
! allowed hosts - and it blocks requests and cookies itself, by consent. ui.js
! is the banner, 390kB of Preact, which airgap loads only when it decides to
! prompt.
!
! So airgap.js is deliberately left alone: with a refusal recorded it is the
! thing enforcing it. Replacing it would drop that blocking and mean
! reimplementing the API it exposes.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||transcend-cdn.com/cm/*/ui.js$script,redirect=transcend-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Served first-party.
!
! A tenant may serve the same files from its own host by CNAME - Costco uses
! consent.costco.ca - and those need naming per site:
!
! ||consent.costco.ca/cm/*/ui.js$script,redirect=transcend-reject.js
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Sites with a consent UI of their own.
!
! A site may build its own banner on airgap rather than use ui.js - Costco's
! costco-consent-manager.iife.js does, and calls setConsent(null, ...) itself,
! which is how we know a tenant with auth off takes a decision that was not
! made by clicking.
!
! Such a banner reads the same refusal, so it has nothing to prompt for - but it
! may ask before airgap has got as far as loading ui.js. Injecting the scriptlet
! at document_start records the refusal before anything renders, and the
! redirect above then has nothing left to do:
!
! costco.ca##+js(transcend-reject)
! costco.com##+js(transcend-reject)
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! ui.js is what asks for these, so with the banner replaced they are not
! fetched. The cross-domain frame is what syncs a decision between a tenant's
! domains; with a refusal recorded, a refusal is what it syncs.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||transcend-cdn.com/cm/*/metadata.json$xhr,redirect=noopjson
