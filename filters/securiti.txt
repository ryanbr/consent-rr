! Title: consent-rr - Securiti
! Description: Replaces Securiti's cookie-consent-sdk-loader.js with a stub that
!              reports a refusal, instead of waiting for a banner.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! The loader is the bootstrapper: it asks app.securiti.ai where the visitor is,
! decides whether TCF applies, and then loads the SDK - 600kB of it - with its
! CSS, its utils and the site's configuration. Replacing the loader means none
! of that is requested.
!
! Their loader takes its CDN base from its own src, so a tenant served from
! another Securiti host needs that host adding here.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||cdn-prod.securiti.ai/consent/cookie-consent-sdk-loader.js$script,redirect=securiti-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Where a redirect cannot land.
!
! A user resource is served as a data: URI, so a site whose CSP omits data: for
! scripts refuses it - nothing of ours runs, no cookie, no console line, while
! the banner is gone anyway because the redirect kept the loader out. The
! scriptlet form is injected rather than fetched, so the directive does not
! apply to it. Both lines are needed on such a site:
!
! example.com##+js(securiti-reject)
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Leave their auto-blocking script alone.
!
! A site may load a second, per-tenant file beside the loader:
!
!   cdn-app3.securiti.ai/consent/auto_blocking/<tenant>/<domain>.js
!
! It blocks tags by the site's own classification - moving src to data-src and
! the type to text/plain - and releases a category when the SDK calls
! setConsentedCategories. With the loader replaced that call never comes, and
! its own rule is
!
!   allowed = consented ids + non_optout_categories   (which is Essential alone)
!
! so it reads the refusal this writes, releases nothing, and leaves essential
! scripts running. That is the refusal enforced by the site's own list, for
! free, so blocking or nooping this file makes things worse rather than better.
!
! One caveat: it reads the consent cookie when it loads, which can be before
! the loader runs. A visitor who had previously accepted gets one more page
! load on the old cookie before this takes over.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! The loader is what asks for these, so with it replaced they are never
! requested. They only matter where something unblocks it: the SDK itself, its
! utils and stylesheet, the IAB stub, and the location lookup it will not start
! without.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||cdn-prod.securiti.ai/consent/cookie-consent-sdk-*.js$script,redirect=noopjs
||cdn-prod.securiti.ai/consent/sdk-stub.js$script,redirect=noopjs
||cdn-prod.securiti.ai/consent/cookie-consent-*.css$stylesheet,redirect=noopcss
||app.securiti.ai/core/v1/utils/geo/location$xhr,redirect=noopjson
