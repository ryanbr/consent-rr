! Title: consent-rr - Termly
! Description: Replaces Termly's resource-blocker with a stub that records
!              their own refusal and leaves their parked tags parked.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! One file is their whole CMP - the auto-blocker, the banner, the tenant
! configuration and the visitor's geo, around 460kB of it, served per request.
! The path carries the website uuid and the options arrive as a query string,
! autoBlock=on and sometimes masterConsentsOrigin for a group of sites sharing
! one consent, so the rule stops at the path.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||app.termly.io/resource-blocker/$script,redirect=termly-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Where a redirect cannot land.
!
! A user resource is served as a data: URI, so a site whose CSP omits data: for
! scripts refuses it - nothing of ours runs, while the banner is gone anyway
! because the redirect kept their file out. The scriptlet form is injected
! rather than fetched, so the directive does not apply to it:
!
! example.com##+js(termly-reject)
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Their embed widget and documents are left alone.
!
!   app.termly.io/embed.min.js
!   app.termly.io/document/<kind>/<uuid>
!
! Those render a published privacy policy or cookie list into the page, which
! is content a visitor asked for rather than consent machinery, and blocking
! them leaves a hole in the page without refusing anything.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
