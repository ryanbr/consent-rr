! Title: consent-rr - AppConsent
! Description: Replaces AppConsent's core bundle with a stub that answers
!              __tcfapi and writes their IABTCF_ keys.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! The core bundle is their whole CMP: the TCF API with the IAB's own cmpapi
! embedded, their state, and the loader for the banner chunks it fetches
! afterwards. The path carries either "current" or a version.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||cdn.appconsent.io/tcf2-clear/*/core.bundle.js$script,redirect=appconsent-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Those chunks - 1284, 5572, 6648 and 807 in the version sampled - are the
! banner. The core is what asks for them, so with it replaced none is
! requested; this line only matters where something else does.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||cdn.appconsent.io/tcf2-clear/*/*.bundle.js$script,redirect=noopjs

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! For a site that makes a refusal cost something.
!
! appconsent-accept.js grants: every purpose, every legitimate interest, both
! special features and every vendor, in the string and in their IABTCF_ keys.
! It is for a consent-or-pay wall that keeps the page shut until the answer is
! yes - and the trade is real, because a vendor handed that string is entitled
! to act on it. uBlock Origin still filters what those vendors request, which
! is the only reason this is worth having.
!
! Per site, and never globally:
!
! ||cdn.appconsent.io/tcf2-clear/*/core.bundle.js$script,redirect=appconsent-accept.js:10,domain=example.com
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
