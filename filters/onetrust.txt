! Title: consent-rr - OneTrust
! Description: Replaces OneTrust's consent SDK with a stub that reports an
!              already-made decision, instead of waiting for a banner to render
!              and clicking it.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: they name the resources
! dist/onetrust-reject.js and dist/onetrust-accept.js, which have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first -
! either one on its own, or both. See the README.
!
! uBlock Origin only (the MV2 extension). uBO Lite cannot load user resources.
!
! Matched by filename rather than by host and path, because none of the three
! are fixed: the stub rewrites its own URL for migrated tenants, to
! /scripttemplates/new/scripttemplates/otSDKStub.js or an old/ equivalent;
! OneTrust serves it from regional CDNs as well as cdn.cookielaw.org and
! cookiepro.com; and some sites proxy it first-party. Host-anchored rules on
! /scripttemplates/otSDKStub.js miss all three - checked against uBO's own
! filtering engine, not assumed.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Reject all - C0001 only, everything else off, and IAB TCF vendors told no.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
/otSDKStub.js$script,redirect=onetrust-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Accept all - for sites that hide content behind a category rather than behind
! the banner. The higher redirect priority beats the reject rule above, so add a
! domain here instead of excepting it.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! /otSDKStub.js$script,redirect=onetrust-accept.js:10,domain=example.com

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! The stub above is what asks for these, so they are only reached when something
! unblocks the stub itself. OtAutoBlock.js is the part that parks third-party
! tags at type="text/plain"; neutering it leaves them to load on their own,
! which is what the accept-all resource would have done anyway.
!
! Where another list excepts the stub for a site, a plain redirect loses to that
! exception - uBO only consults the block-important realm once an exception has
! matched. Intercepting one step later, at the banner SDK, needs `important`:
!   /otBannerSdk.js$script,redirect=onetrust-reject.js,important,domain=example.com
! The real stub will have run by then, and the stub's parked __tcfapi calls are
! replayed, but its two data layer load events fire twice.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
/otBannerSdk.js$script,redirect=noopjs
/OtAutoBlock.js$script,redirect=noopjs

! Sites that keep their own record of the choice are handled by the resources
! themselves, which set localStorage cookieChoiceMade. A site using some other
! key of its own needs a per-site rule here:
!   example.com##+js(set-local-storage-item, cookieConsentGiven, true)
