! Title: consent-rr - Cookiez
! Description: Replaces the Cookiez WordPress plugin's banner bundle with
!              their own refusal, carrying the hash their gate checks.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! A WORDPRESS PLUGIN, so like Complianz it is served FIRST-PARTY from the
! site's own wp-content path and there is no vendor host to match:
!
!   /wp-content/plugins/cookiez/assets/build/banner.js?ver=<hash>
!   /wp-content/plugins/cookiez/assets/build/lang-en.js?ver=<hash>
!
! The rule matches the file under any cookiez* plugin directory, which is what
! keeps working when a premium or renamed build turns up, and the ?ver= query
! does not get in the way.
!
! Swap in cookiez-reject-unblock.js on a site that withholds something until a
! category is on.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
/plugins/cookiez*/assets/build/banner.js$script,redirect=cookiez-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! THEIR LANGUAGE FILE IS A WEBPACK CHUNK of banner text, pushed into
! globalThis.webpackChunkcookiez by the bundle's own chunk loader - so with
! banner.js replaced nothing ever asks for it. The rule below is for a page
! that hardcodes it anyway, and noopjs is enough because no banner is built.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
/plugins/cookiez*/assets/build/lang-*.js$script,redirect=noopjs

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! THEIR RECORD, reproduced field for field from a real refusal on a site
! running it:
!
!   cookiez-user-consent={"data":{"consent":{"necessary":true,
!       "functional":false,"analytics":false,"advertising":false,
!       "unclassified":false}},
!       "meta":{"cookiesHash":"...","timestamp":1791239063}}
!
! URI-encoded, path=/, max-age 86400 * consentExpiration and SameSite=Lax, all
! from their own writer. Five categories, and their builder is
! J = e => Object.fromEntries(Object.values(R).map(t => [t, t === Necessary || e]))
! so J(false) is exactly the refusal above.
!
! THE HASH DECIDES WHETHER THE RECORD COUNTS. Their gate throws a record away
! when meta.cookiesHash does not match window.cookiezBannerSettings.cookiesHash,
! or when it is older than consentExpiration days - and then the banner shows
! again. The hash is a page global, which is the only reason a record written
! from outside can survive.
!
! THE consentId IS NOT MINTED, and that is not a judgement call here: theirs
! comes back from their own server, and when that call fails their own record
! carries no consentId at all. One already stored is kept; otherwise the key is
! left out, which is a shape their own code produces.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! WHAT THEIR BOOT DOES THAT THE REFUSAL OVERRIDES. On a site configured
! adaptive and opt-in-out, their own code asks their geo endpoint and then, in
! a no-banner region, UN-PARKS EVERYTHING and tells both bridges granted - and
! in an opt-out region tells both bridges granted - with no banner and no user
! action. A record written here overrides that, and stops the geo call
! happening at all next page: their gate is short-circuited by "a record
! exists".
!
! THEIR DNT SUPPORT DOES NOT EXIST. The setting is called gpcDntSupport, and
! doNotTrack appears nowhere in their bundle - only globalPrivacyControl is
! read. Nothing is claimed on DNT's behalf here either.
!
! Deliberately left alone:
!
!   their REST route     theirs posts the decision to
!                        cookiezBannerSettings.serviceUrl with an X-WP-Nonce
!                        header and takes the consentId from the reply.
!                        Nothing is sent.
!   window.cookiezBanner banner internals - screenManager, translations,
!                        content, apiConfig - put up only when a banner is
!                        built. A screenManager of this repo's making would be
!                        worse than its absence, and their bundle dispatches no
!                        events at all, so there are none to reproduce either.
!   data-cc-mode=always  their own never-free marker. Even the unblock variant
!                        leaves those parked, because their own un-parking
!                        excludes them with a :not().
!
! To see it work:
!
!   [consent-rr] cookiez-reject 1.0.0 categories=necessary cookie=written
!                hash=theirs consentid=none told=4 freed=0 gpc=unset
!                banner=none log=none
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
