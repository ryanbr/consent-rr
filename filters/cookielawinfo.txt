! Title: consent-rr - Cookie Law Info (legacy WordPress plugin)
! Description: Replaces the legacy cookie-law-info plugin script with a stub
!              that records their decline and takes their banner out.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! WebToffee's "GDPR Cookie Consent" for WordPress, plugin slug cookie-law-info -
! the ancestor of today's CookieYes plugin. NOT the hosted CookieYes script,
! which is a different generation of the same company's work and has its own
! list here: nothing of _ckyStore, cookieyes-consent or data-cookieyes exists in
! this one.
!
! Every copy is served by the site itself, so there is no host to name - one
! rule by path. Four live examples, all the same asset at different plugin
! versions, and the last two byte-identical to each other:
!
!   www.adamoads.com/wp-content/plugins/cookie-law-info/js/cookielawinfo.js?ver=1.6.3
!   www.wowaura.com/es/wp-content/plugins/cookie-law-info/js/cookielawinfo.js?ver=1.5.4
!   www.kitvideosorveglianza.biz/wp-content/.../cookielawinfo.js?ver=3.3.2
!   7tvregiondemurcia.es/wp-content/.../cookielawinfo.js?ver=4.1.10
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
/plugins/cookie-law-info/js/cookielawinfo.js$script,redirect=cookielawinfo-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! IT CANNOT BE BLOCKED INSTEAD. The page calls their function from an inline
! script, with its own markup and settings as arguments:
!
!   cli_show_cookiebar({
!       html: '<div id="cookie-law-info-bar">...</div>',
!       settings: '{"notify_div_id":"#cookie-law-info-bar", ...}'
!   });
!
! With the file gone that is a ReferenceError, and it takes the rest of that
! inline block with it - on the sites sampled the call sits inside a jQuery
! ready handler shared with other plugins. Their other global, l1hs, goes back
! in for the same reason.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! THEIR BANNER IS NOT ALWAYS THEIRS TO INSERT, which is the thing to know about
! this plugin. 3.3.2 and 4.1.10 prepend the markup themselves -
!
!   jQuery('body').prepend(html);
!
! so replacing the file leaves nothing in the document. 1.5.4 and 1.6.3 have no
! prepend at all, and no mention of body: their markup is printed into the page
! by PHP, and the script only wires and positions it. A live 1.6.3 page carries
!
!   <div id="cookie-law-info-bar">...</div>
!   <div id="cookie-law-info-again">...</div>
!
! and their own css gives the bar position:absolute and z-index:9999 with NO
! display:none - it is their script that hides it once the cookie exists. So a
! stub that only declined would leave a dead cookie bar on the page.
!
! Both of theirs are taken out, by the ids their own settings name -
! notify_div_id and showagain_div_id - and by their defaults where the page
! never calls in. Removed rather than hidden: with no banner to reopen, a
! show-again tab that does nothing is worse than no tab.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! THEIR RECORD is one cookie, and their own constants:
!
!   ACCEPT_COOKIE_NAME = 'viewed_cookie_policy'
!   ACCEPT_COOKIE_EXPIRE = 365
!   document.cookie = name+"="+value+expires+"; path=/";
!
! with 'yes' from their accept and 'no' from their decline - which only 1.5.4
! and 1.6.3 write, the later file having no decline path at all. 'no' is what
! goes in, because it is their own value for this answer, and a stored 'yes'
! from before this was installed is overwritten. Their banner shows on that
! cookie being absent rather than on its value.
!
! To see it work:
!
!   [consent-rr] cookielawinfo-reject 1.0.0 cookie=viewed_cookie_policy=no
!                stored=yes was=absent removed=2 banner=none blocked=none
!                sent=none
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! ONE RESOURCE, because that is the whole plugin: not one script is blocked or
! parked by any version of it, no category is named, and there is no api beyond
! the banner. There is nothing to un-block and nothing to accept.
!
! Their stylesheet is left alone - css/cli-style.css styles a bar that is no
! longer there, and blocking it would be one more request for no reason.
!
! WHAT THIS DOES NOT COVER: the modern plugin's own script,
! cookie-law-info-public.js, which does park scripts - data-cli-class,
! text/plain - and writes cookielawinfo-checkbox-<category> cookies beside this
! one. None of the four sites sampled ships it; a site that does needs more
! than this resource.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
