! Title: consent-rr - consentmanager.net
! Description: Replaces consentmanager.net's bootstrap and per-tenant delivery
!              with their own refusal, and answers the four APIs they put up.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! WHAT A PAGE ACTUALLY DOWNLOADS, measured on two tenants - fasthosts.co.uk and
! dastelefonbuch.de:
!
!   delivery/js/semiautomatic.min.js    18KB   the bootstrap: it builds the
!                                              cmp.php url from window globals
!                                              and loads the bundle
!   delivery/cmp.php?cdid=...&h=...      6KB   per tenant: window
!                                              .cmp_config_data, the loader
!                                              functions, and the request for
!                                              the custom data
!   delivery/js/cmp_final.min.js       493KB   the CMP itself
!   delivery/customdata/<base64>.js  43-147KB  per tenant vendor and UI data
!
! Replacing the bootstrap or cmp.php takes the rest with it - better than half a
! megabyte a visitor stops fetching. The || rules match every delivery host they
! use: cdn., b.delivery., and the rest.
!
! Swap in consentmanager-reject-unblock.js on a site that withholds something
! until a purpose is consented to: same refusal, and the tags their blocker
! parked are let go.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||consentmanager.net/delivery/js/semiautomatic.min.js$script,redirect=consentmanager-reject.js
||consentmanager.net/delivery/cmp.php$script,redirect=consentmanager-reject.js
||consentmanager.net/delivery/js/cmp_final.min.js$script,redirect=consentmanager-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! THE RECORD THAT MATTERS IS NOT THEIRS. Their own consent record is
! __cmpconsent<id>, __cmpconsentx<id> or __cmpconsents<id>, keyed on a
! consentscope with a per-tenant id - and both live in cmp_config_data inside
! the file being replaced, so the name cannot be known from outside. Nothing
! else reads it: grepped across their bootstrap, their cmp.php and both
! tenants' customdata, the only reader is the 493KB bundle. So it is not
! written, and an invented name would be a record of this repo's making.
!
! What a third party reads is euconsent-v2, which their own bundle writes -
!
!   r.alt = o > 0 ? (s ? "euconsent-v2" : "nc_euconsent-v2")
!                 : (s ? "euconsent" : "nc_euconsent");
!
! so that one IS written here, with a string that grants nothing, as cmpId 31.
! Their own cmp.php says "iabid":31 and the IAB's published list agrees.
!
! EVERYTHING PAGE-FACING HANGS OFF cmpmngr.api, which is their own layout:
! window.__cmp is a thin delegate to window.cmpmngr.api.__cmp, and __tcfapi,
! __gpp and __dsa are the same. Their thirty-command table is answered, and
! each command answers ITS OWN payload - getCMPData has 34 keys, getConsentData
! has three, getVendorConsents carries a custom pair getCMPData does not,
! getVendorList answers {}, and a ping of any version but 2 answers false.
!
! THEIR EVENTS GO TO TWO DIFFERENT TARGETS, which is the sort of thing that
! quietly reaches nobody if it is assumed: cmpEvent and cmpEvent_<type> at the
! WINDOW, and their WordPress Consent API bridge, wp_consent_type_defined, at
! the DOCUMENT - next to window.wp_consent_type and a wp_set_consent per
! category.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Deliberately left alone:
!
!   delivery/customdata/*    per-tenant vendor and UI data. With cmp.php
!                            replaced nothing asks for it, and the file NAME is
!                            itself the configuration: base64 of
!                            m_1.w_170577.r_GDPR.l_en.d_53863.x_130... so it
!                            carries the website id, the regime and the
!                            language.
!   their blocker            it is in the bundle being replaced. uBlock Origin
!                            is what blocks once it is gone, and what their
!                            markup parked stays parked under reject.
!   their cross-domain frame theirs posts cmpcd:set:<key>=<value> into a
!                            __cmpcdframe iframe to share a choice between a
!                            customer's domains. Nothing is shared from here.
!   their tracking           theirs calls Microsoft Clarity, Microsoft UET and
!                            Xandr, and pushes a data layer event, on a
!                            decision. None of it is sent.
!
!
! THE PASS THAT FREES PARKED TAGS RUNS AGAIN, because at document_start the
! tags are not there yet: this resource stands in for a script in <head>, and
! everything the page parks for them is below it. So the pass runs on boot, on
! every batch of nodes the parser delivers, at DOMContentLoaded and at load.
! The boot line below therefore says freed=0 on a real page, and a later pass
! that frees something says so on its own line:
!
!   [consent-rr] consentmanager-reject-unblock 1.1.0 freed=1 deferred
!
! To see it work:
!
!   [consent-rr] consentmanager-reject 1.0.0 purposes=none vendors=none
!                api=__cmp+__uspapi tcf=refused freed=0 events=5 told=5
!                banner=none crossdomain=none tracking=none
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
