! Title: consent-rr - CookieScript
! Description: Replaces CookieScript's per-site bundle with a stub that records
!              their own reject-all and leaves their parked tags parked.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! One per-site bundle is the whole CMP - the banner, the auto-blocker, the
! tenant's configuration and the loader for their IAB SDK. The path carries a
! hash of the site's own configuration.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||cdn.cookie-script.com/s/*.js$script,redirect=cookiescript-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Their IAB SDK, which the bundle fetches only for a TCF tenant. With the
! bundle replaced nothing asks for it.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||cdn.cookie-script.com/iabtcf/*/sdk_cmp.js$script,redirect=noopjs

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! The vendor lists are left alone.
!
!   cdn.cookie-script.com/iabtcf/<version>/vendor-list.json
!   cdn.cookie-script.com/iabtcf/<version>/google-vendors.json
!
! The SDK is what asks for them, so with it kept out neither is requested. A
! rule for them would only matter on a site whose CMP still runs, and there it
! would break the banner rather than refuse anything.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Where a redirect cannot land, the scriptlet form covers it:
!
! example.com##+js(cookiescript-reject)
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
