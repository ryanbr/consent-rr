! Title: consent-rr - Complianz
! Description: Replaces Complianz's cookiebanner script with a stub that
!              records their own refusal and leaves their parked tags parked.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Complianz is a WordPress plugin, so unlike every other CMP here it is served
! FIRST-PARTY from the site's own wp-content path. There is no vendor host to
! match, and the plugin directory is not one name but several:
!
!   /wp-content/plugins/complianz-gdpr/cookiebanner/js/complianz.min.js
!   /wp-content/plugins/complianz-gdpr-premium/cookiebanner/js/complianz.min.js
!   /wp-content/plugins/fhoke-support-1.1.0/dist/js/complianz.min.js
!
! the third being a theme that vendors their banner under its own name. So the
! rule matches the FILE under any plugins path, which is what keeps working
! when the next theme does the same thing.
!
! Swap in complianz-accept.js on a site that breaks under a refusal.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
/wp-content/plugins/*/complianz.min.js$script,redirect=complianz-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! NOTHING IN THEIR RECORD CAN BE HARDCODED, which is worth knowing before
! reading the resource. Every cookie name is complianz.prefix + name, and the
! prefix, the expiry, the policy id, the consent type, the region and the
! cookie domain and path all come from a `complianz` object the page declares
! before their script would have run. The resource reads that object.
!
! Three of their behaviours the resource has to follow:
!
!   AN EMPTY COOKIE CAN MEAN YES. Their cmplz_has_consent treats an absent
!   record as consent where complianz.consenttype is "optout" or "other", so a
!   refusal writes "deny" explicitly. Writing nothing would consent on exactly
!   the sites that assume consent.
!
!   THE POLICY ID IS PART OF THE RECORD. Their cmplz_check_cookie_policy_id
!   compares the stored id against complianz.current_policy_id and, on a
!   mismatch, denies everything, clears every cmplz cookie and shows the
!   banner again. A record without the current id is wiped on the next page.
!
!   THEY DISPATCH PER CATEGORY. cmplz_set_consent ends in a cmplz_status_change
!   CustomEvent carrying { category, value, region, categories }, once per
!   category, and both page code and their tag-manager bridge listen for it.
!
! Their do-not-track handling is left alone: cmplz_do_not_track() reads
! navigator.globalPrivacyControl and navigator.doNotTrack, and a visitor who
! set either has already said something this should not talk over.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Deliberately left alone:
!
!   /wp-json/complianz/      their REST routes, which the banner script calls.
!                            With it replaced nothing calls them.
!   complianz-gpc.min.js     their Global Privacy Control helper, which is on
!                            the visitor's side.
!
! To see it work, the console line names what it wrote and what it read from
! the page's own configuration:
!
!   [consent-rr] complianz-reject 1.0.0 categories=functional cookies=4
!                services=2 revived=0 told=4 prefix=cmplz_ policy=kept
!                consenttype=optin
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
