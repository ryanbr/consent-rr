! Title: consent-rr - Ketch
! Description: Replaces Ketch's boot.js with a stub that answers their command
!              queue with a refusal, and keeps their SDK out.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! boot.js is the loader: it inlines the property's configuration and a
! country-to-jurisdiction table, puts their command queue on the page, and then
! fetches the SDK. The path carries the organization and the property.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||global.ketchcdn.com/web/v3/config/*/boot.js$script,redirect=ketch-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Their SDK, 1.9MB of it, which the loader would have fetched. With the loader
! replaced nothing asks for it, so this line only matters where a property
! names it directly - and there it would start without the init entry their
! queue needs and throw.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||cdn.ketchjs.com/ketchtag/*/ketch-sdk.js$script,redirect=noopjs

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! The config and the geo lookup are left alone.
!
!   global.ketchcdn.com/web/v3/config/<org>/<property>/<env>/.../config.json
!   global.ketchcdn.com/web/v3/ip
!
! The SDK is what asks for both, so with it kept out neither is requested. A
! rule for them would only matter on a property whose SDK still runs, and there
! it would break the banner rather than refuse anything.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! For a site that withholds content until a purpose is consented to.
!
! ketch-reject-unblock.js stores and sends the same refusal as the resource
! above - their record denied, their Google consent mode denied - while the API
! answers the page that every purpose is consented, so the content is released.
! The cost is precisely that: whatever the site had withheld now runs, and
! uBlock Origin is what filters its requests.
!
! realtruck.com gates video on consent.purposes.optional, read straight out of
! their own bundle:
!
!   window.ketch("on", "consent", e => { hasConsent = e?.purposes?.optional })
!
! so the unblock resource is what releases it there. The higher redirect
! priority is what makes it win over the general rule above.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||global.ketchcdn.com/web/v3/config/*/boot.js$script,redirect=ketch-reject-unblock.js:10,domain=realtruck.com

! Any other site that withholds content the same way:
! ||global.ketchcdn.com/web/v3/config/*/boot.js$script,redirect=ketch-reject-unblock.js:10,domain=example.com

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Where a redirect cannot land, or a property loads the SDK directly without
! the loader, the scriptlet form covers it:
!
! example.com##+js(ketch-reject)
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
