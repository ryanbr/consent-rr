! Title: consent-rr - PubTech CMP
! Description: Replaces PubTech's CMP with a stub that records a refusal in
!              their own publisher-cookie string and TC string.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! One file is the whole CMP: the TCF API, the banner, the vendor lists it goes
! on to fetch, and the tenant's own configuration, which is inlined at the top
! of that very file as window.__pub_tech_cmp_config. The path carries the
! tenant id - 312, 466, 121, 356 and 188 seen in the field - and they ship two
! builds under two names, pubtech-cmp-v2-esm.js and pubtech-cmp-v2.js, so both
! parts are wildcarded.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||cmp.pubtech.ai/*/pubtech-cmp-*.js$script,redirect=pubtech-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Their module build is one of two: pubtech-cmp-v2-esm.js is loaded as
! <script type="module" src="...">, while pubtech-cmp-v2.js is an ordinary
! script and has no such question.
!
! A user resource is served as a data: URI, and if a browser declines to run a
! module from one then nothing of ours runs. So on a module tenant the
! scriptlet form is the reliable shape: it is injected rather than fetched, and
! runs at document_start, before their bundle would have.
!
! example.com##+js(pubtech-reject)
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Their asset host is deliberately left alone.
!
!   cmp-assets.pubtech.ai/vendorList/v2.2/<version>/vendor-list.json
!   cmp-assets.pubtech.ai/vendorList/v2.2/<version>/google-vendor-list.json
!   cmp-assets.pubtech.ai/<tenant>/<folder>/<lang>/cookie/<n>/publisher-cookie.json
!
! The CMP is what asks for all three - the IAB vendor list at the version
! pinned in its bundle, Google's additional-consent list, and the publisher's
! own cookie declarations - so with it replaced they are never requested. A
! rule for them would only matter on a page where their CMP is still running,
! and there it would break the banner rather than the tracking.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
