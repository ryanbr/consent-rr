! Title: consent-rr - tarteaucitron
! Description: Replaces tarteaucitron's core with their own refusal, and
!              optionally keeps the videos and social embeds working.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! TWO DEPLOYMENTS. tarteaucitron is both an open-source CMP a site hosts itself
! and a paid service at tarteaucitron.io, so there is a vendor host to match AND
! a file to match anywhere:
!
!   cdntag.tarteaucitron.io/load.js?domain=...&uuid=...   the hosted loader: one
!       95KB file with the site's configuration and its service list inlined.
!
!   www.info.gouv.fr/build/tarteaucitron.<hash>.js        self-hosted, under any
!       path and usually with a build hash. There the PAGE calls
!       tarteaucitron.init({...}) and pushes its own services, which arrive
!       after the resource and are handled as they come.
!
! Swap in tarteaucitron-reject-unblock.js to keep the videos and the social
! embeds working - see the README, it consents to those two types only.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||cdntag.tarteaucitron.io/load.js$script,redirect=tarteaucitron-reject.js
/tarteaucitron.js$script,redirect=tarteaucitron-reject.js
/tarteaucitron.min.js$script,redirect=tarteaucitron-reject.js
/\/tarteaucitron\.[0-9a-f]{6,}\.(min\.)?js/$script,redirect=tarteaucitron-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! THEIR SERVICES BUNDLE AND LANGUAGE FILE ARE NOT MATCHED, on purpose, and the
! two patterns above are written so they cannot catch them:
!
!   cdn.tarteaucitron.io/tarteaucitron.services.min.js?v=1.35.0
!   cdn.tarteaucitron.io/lang/tarteaucitron.fr.min.js?v=1.35.0
!
! Neither runs anything on its own - the first is a registry of 247 service
! definitions, the second is banner text - and the registry is what the
! reject-unblock variant needs: a service's launcher is
! tarteaucitron.services[key].js(), so without it a consented video cannot be
! built. Redirecting it would make that variant a no-op.
!
! THE RECORD IS ONE COOKIE, named by their configuration and defaulting to
! tarteaucitron, with an entry per service:
!
!   tarteaucitron=!youtube=true!googleads=false!xiti=false
!
! measured by driving their own Deny All, which leaves exactly that shape.
! Their per-service events go to the DOCUMENT - youtube_consentModeOk,
! googleads_consentModeKo - and are not decoration: their own Google, Bing and
! Clarity glue listens for them, so a refusal that skipped them would stop at
! the cookie and never reach consent mode. Consent mode itself is pushed in the
! shape theirs pushes - an arguments object, keys "0","1","2", not an array -
! because that is what Tag Manager reads a consent command out of.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Deliberately left alone:
!
!   no banner      nothing is built, so there is no external CSS to fetch.
!   no reload      their respondAll sets reloadThePage when a refusal revokes
!                  something already launched. Nothing launches under reject,
!                  so there is nothing to undo.
!   no beacon      their pro() posts the visitor's choice to
!                  logs.tarteaucitron.io/collect with the site's uuid and
!                  domain, through sendBeacon. Not sent.
!
! To see it work:
!
!   [consent-rr] tarteaucitron-reject 1.0.0 refused=2 allowed=0 launched=0
!                cookie=tarteaucitron entries=2 events=5 banner=none reload=no
!                beacon=none
!
!   [consent-rr] tarteaucitron-reject-unblock 1.0.0 refused=1 allowed=2
!                launched=2 types=video+social cookie=tarteaucitron entries=3
!                events=6 banner=none reload=no beacon=none
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
