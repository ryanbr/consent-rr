! Title: consent-rr - Osano
! Description: Replaces Osano's osano.js with a stub that reports a refusal,
!              instead of waiting for a banner.
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/ryanbr/consent-rr
!
! These filters do nothing on their own: the resources they name have to be
! installed through uBlock Origin's userResourcesLocation setting first. See the
! README.
!
! Host-anchored, like the Cookie Information list. Osano serves the whole CMP -
! configuration, translations and all - from one per-tenant file under
! cmp.osano.com/<customer>/<config>/osano.js, so there is one thing to replace
! and one host to match on.

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! The essential category accepted, everything else refused. One resource: no
! accept-all or unblock variant for this consent manager.
!
! Second line: their loader also builds per-tenant variants of the same file,
! osano-<name>.js and its .legacy and .modern forms.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||cmp.osano.com/*/osano.js$script,redirect=osano-reject.js
||cmp.osano.com/*/osano-*.js$script,redirect=osano-reject.js

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Where a redirect cannot land.
!
! A user resource is served as a data: URI, so a site whose CSP omits data: for
! scripts refuses it - nothing of ours runs, no cookie, no console line, while
! the banner is gone anyway because the redirect kept the real CMP out. The
! scriptlet form is injected rather than fetched, so the directive does not apply
! to it. Both lines are needed on such a site:
!
! example.com##+js(osano-reject)
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! osano.js is what asks for these, so with it replaced they are never requested.
! They only matter where something unblocks osano.js itself: the consent record
! it posts back, the blocked-item telemetry, the jurisdiction lookup, the
! per-category disclosure lists and the translations.
! -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
||consent.api.osano.com^$xhr,redirect=noopjson
||tattle.api.osano.com^$xhr,redirect=noopjson
||lookups.api.osano.com^$xhr,redirect=noopjson
||disclosure.api.osano.com^$xhr,redirect=noopjson
||locale.cmp.osano.com^$xhr,redirect=noopjson
